## 2026-09-25 — clarified failure criterion

### Key insight
The user does **not** require a weak protagonist. An OP protagonist is fine. The important requirement is that the protagonist can genuinely lose/fail repeatedly.

Time-loop stories are especially strong because death can function as a real failure state without ending the narrative. Preferred failures involve being outplayed, making a wrong decision, failing an objective, losing a fight/person/opportunity, or discovering that an approach was fundamentally wrong. The best progression from a loss often includes information/understanding and a changed strategy, but mechanical progression from death is also acceptable.

## 2026-09-27 — corrected Royal Road-only search

### Current hard filters
- Royal Road only.
- Human male MC only.
- ACTIVE or COMPLETED only; silently discard hiatus/inactive/stubbed stories.
- Medieval/high-fantasy core setting preferred.
- Strict no-spoiler output: later material may be checked privately, but do not reveal future deaths, counts, arc outcomes, identities, betrayals, powers, relationship turns, or discoveries.
- Death granting XP/stats/skills is allowed. Judge whether failure/tension still matters; do not reject the mechanic by itself.

- Re-evaluated because an earlier rejection was based on the mistaken blanket anti-death-progression rule.
- Royal Road original, ongoing; 47 chapters / roughly 511 pages as of Sept 27, 2026; about M-W-F cadence.
- Human male MC, medieval/high-fantasy battlefield setting, checkpoint-style time loop.
- Strong evidence from reviews that the MC repeatedly fails and tries alternate approaches; loops skip repetitive material rather than narrating every identical attempt.
- Progression does carry across deaths, but readers still describe the MC as outmatched at points and the story as tense rather than effortless.
- Strongest caveat for this user: it is heavily combat-focused. Critical reviews say human interaction/world outside violence can feel thin, and one reviewer specifically felt the certainty of power growth reduced stakes. This may be less mystery/politics/relationship-driven than Hundred Reigns or Re:ZERO.
- Other reviews praise grounded action, serious tone, side characters, lore, and lack of repetitive loop narration.
