## Search target
- Human male MC.
- Royal Road only.
- Active or completed only.
- Prefer medieval/high fantasy.
- Strong preference for immediate narrative movement, dangerous opposition, meaningful setbacks, mystery/world discovery, and no early glazing/chosen-one validation.
- Time loop/regression still preferred, but the current pool is heavily exhausted; non-loop wildcards are acceptable only if they are exciting and otherwise strong fits.

## Strongest new candidate: The Blessed War
- Royal Road original, ONGOING.
- 41 chapters / ~230 pages at current crawl; Book 1 complete and Book 2 ongoing.
- Daily releases; author states three books are already written with 100+ chapters backlog.
- Human male MC Ren Aurvessa, age 13.
- High-fantasy martial/progression setting; no harem.
- MC begins explicitly as the family disappointment with a defective/weak blessing rather than an obvious prodigy.
- Opening inspected: first chapter is family/ceremony/sword-training setup, but the inciting disaster begins within the opening sequence and the LitRPG/progression mechanism appears by chapter 5.
- Progression has finite Life/Breath/Spirit pools and injuries/resource costs are intended to matter. Monsters have rules to learn and exploit.
- Strong evidence of real danger and physical consequences; early power does not simply erase injury or make the MC dominant.
- Prose in opening is restrained and controlled; no obvious AI warning surfaced.
- Caveats: grimdark, young MC, no time loop, and first 4 chapters are deliberate setup before the progression system fully appears.
- Fit estimate: ~8/10 if the user tolerates dark tone and young MC.

## Secondary candidate: The First Runebound Mercenary
- Royal Road original, ONGOING.
- 19 chapters / ~141 pages; very new.
- Human male MC Atreus, low-ranking mercenary/scout.
- Opens directly with an expedition into ancient ruins; prior scouts have vanished, and Atreus knows he is taking a mission above his station for money to treat his mother.
- Opening inspected: he encounters enemies he explicitly cannot beat, is badly injured, and gains rune magic only after near-fatal failure. His new spell can save him once but immediately fails as an easy repeat solution due to depleted resources.
- Hidden-magic conspiracy / ancient-order mystery is the core hook; progression is promised to be slow, with permanent rune choices and months required to master new magic.
- No early crowd-glazing in the first chapters.
- Caveats: extremely new/thin backlog, generic LitRPG system UI appears early, and confidence is low because there is little later material/review evidence yet.
- Do not overstate; present as a promising secondary/watchlist candidate rather than a strong established rec.
