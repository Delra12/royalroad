## Strong recommendation: A Requiem for Kings (formerly/also marketed as Anciens Dieux)
- Royal Road original, ONGOING as of Oct. 3, 2026.
- Current page: ~18 chapters / ~135 pages. Author says the story is almost fully written and is publishing 4–7 chapters/week (guaranteed M/W/F/Su).
- Native Gothic/low-fantasy realm inspired by medieval France; no LitRPG/stat sheets.
- Central revenge/political thread follows Duke Ríal Delois, a human male noble whose son was murdered by the Mad King. Current published POVs inspected (Damien, Gabriel, Jacques, Ríal, Simon) are male; story is explicitly ensemble/multiple-lead rather than single-MC.
- Strongest fit axis: multiple noble houses, court factions, a deranged supernaturally empowered king, ancient gods, cursed knights, competing conspirators, and a major supernatural threat all acting independently. This naturally creates the same kind of 'important established players collide' potential the user highlighted with Oracle/Elios Magnus in Hundred Reigns.
- Opening chapters inspected directly. Prose is distinctive and controlled, not generic RR/AI cadence. Damien is experienced but frightened/outmatched; Gabriel is a duke dealing with a threat bigger than his domain; Jacques is a court operator around the Mad King. Ríal enters as a politically important duke rather than an instantly validated weak prodigy.
- Author explicitly promises no plot armor / no one safe. Current material supports genuine danger and costs, but because only ~18 chapters of the rewrite are live there is not enough long-run published evidence to score repeated MC failure at Hundred Reigns/Re:Zero levels.
- The story has been in development for years: the author's Royal Road forum posts show Anciens Dieux attached to their account as far back as 2021/2023, reducing concern that the current page is a newly improvised AI serial. No RR AI-Assisted/AI-Generated warning surfaced.
- Caveats: grimdark/cosmic horror/body horror is much heavier than Hundred Reigns; ensemble structure rather than one dominant MC; not progression-focused or a time loop; current rewrite is still early on RR.
- Best framing: strongest recent find for the Oracle/Elios 'major players with independent agendas colliding' preference, weaker proof for repeated personal failure.
