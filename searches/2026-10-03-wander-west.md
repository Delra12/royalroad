## Strong recommendation: Wander West, in Shadow — CloverCloverClover
- Royal Road original, ONGOING, final polished rewrite of Book 1.
- Current final rewrite: 26 chapters / ~553 RR pages; Book 1 finished Oct. 3, 2026. Page states M/W/F during release. Author says this is the final polished version before ebook release later in 2026.
- Central protagonist is Martimeos, a human young male wizard searching for his missing brother after a devastating war. Royal Road tags Male Lead + Multiple Lead Characters; Elyse, a female witch, is a very prominent companion / secondary lead-level character. This is the main caveat against the user's usual single-male-MC preference, though a current review explicitly calls Martimeos the clear MC.
- Native high-fantasy world; no LitRPG/system/isekai/reincarnation. Romance is a subplot and intentionally slowed down by the author.
- No AI-Assisted/AI-Generated warning surfaced. Author explicitly describes years of rewriting/polishing and a final edit for ebook release.
- Chapter 1 inspected directly. Prose is polished, atmospheric, deliberate and clearly not generic AI-flavored prose. Martimeos is already modestly trained rather than a fake-zero-to-secret-god setup. He enters a dangerous magical encounter where his knowledge/preparation are all that keep him alive; he is visibly afraid and recognizes that he may have made a lethal mistake.
- Current reviews praise deep mysteries, subtle lore through songs/riddles/conflicting histories, characters with secrets and independent motivations, and a world that feels as if it existed before the MC and will continue without him.
- Review at current Book 1 ending says there is a clear MC and secondary characters do not merely exist as foils. Another review calls Martimeos and Elyse convincingly imperfect and says even minor characters have personal histories.
- Older-draft reviews remain useful structurally because the author states the new edition preserves the overall structure. They describe Martimeos and Elyse as small fish relative to stronger magical beings and practitioners, often better served by running, hiding, understanding, and adapting rather than confronting threats directly.
- Strong fit for user’s layered-discovery preference: faded kingdoms, old gods, demons, fae, lost history, conflicting versions of the past, mysterious brother, secrets revealed gradually, low-power/esoteric magic, threats that sit well above the protagonist.
- Caveats: not a time loop and not progression-forward in the Royal Road sense; slower old-school fantasy pacing; horror/dark-fantasy atmosphere; Elyse is prominent enough that RR uses Multiple Lead Characters; Book 1 has just finished so future-book cadence is not yet proven.
