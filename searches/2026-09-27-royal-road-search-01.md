# Royal Road Search — 2026-09-27

## Current filters
- Royal Road only.
- Human male MC.
- ACTIVE or COMPLETED only; silently discard inactive/hiatus/stubbed stories.
- Medieval/high-fantasy core setting strongly preferred.
- Repeated meaningful failure/time-loop structure strongly preferred.
- Death granting XP/stats/power is **not** a disqualifier. Judge whether losses still matter and whether uncertainty remains.
- Grounded tone preferred; avoid heavy anime/light-novel tropes, harem-heavy stories, and generic progression sludge.
