## New candidate: Infinite Regressing Knight / Infinite Regression Knight War (무한회귀 기사전)
- IMPORTANT: This is a separate work from **A Knight Who Eternally Regresses / The Knight Only Lives Today**. Do not conflate them.
- Author: Shainak (샤이나크). Protagonist: Dominic Dia.
- Korean web novel based on the Seven Knights IP, but the author explicitly says it was written to stand on its own as a web novel.
- Official Netmarble material says Dominic repeatedly undergoes infinite regression and becomes entangled with different people and events in the Seven Knights world.
- Official author interview describes it as a story about Dominic, who saw himself as a coward/fugitive, gaining infinite regression and pursuing personal growth plus the salvation of the world.
- Setting is fantasy/knights/army-oriented and the author explicitly mentions medieval social structures and a side-character concept involving a poor commoner rising into nobility.
- The author says the time-loop structure repeatedly returns to the beginning and that making Dominic's growth feel clear across repeats was a central writing concern.
- Strong positive for user: unlike many power fantasies, the author says Dominic could not be allowed to grow without limit because he had to remain balanced against established Seven Knights heroes.
- Strong caveat: this is based on a mobile-game IP, so there may be game/anime/Korean-webnovel flavor that the user dislikes. More evidence is needed on actual prose and how much failure/information-gain is shown rather than skipped.
- Completion status: official Netmarble interview says the Korean story **completed at 198 chapters on June 9, 2025**. Some aggregator metadata incorrectly still lists it ongoing at 138 chapters; prefer the official source.
- English availability is messy: Novel Updates historically listed only a small number of fan-translated chapters, while newer aggregator pages show many more recent updates. Do not promise a clean official English edition.

## Considered/rejected this search
- **An Altar on the Village Green** — mechanically excellent: medieval dark fantasy, repeated deaths, mystery, incremental information gain, crushing failures, strong grounded prose. However, the author intentionally leaves the protagonist unnamed, physically undescribed, and ungendered. User requires a clearly human male MC, so do not recommend under current rules.
- **Time Cursed Knight** — loop mechanics, mystery, and smart problem solving are strong, but core setting is explicitly post-apocalyptic future Earth / magitech (2097 and beyond), not medieval fantasy. Also a recent review says the time-loop element largely disappears for ~60 chapters. Reject for setting and sustained-loop mismatch.
- **The Hero Who Returned by Death Wants to Live His Second Life Peacefully** — human male fantasy hero and complete source novel, but it appears to be one major regression/second chance rather than repeated failure loops. Weak match for current target.
- **Return by Death in an Isekai Transfer, I Know the True Nature of All Classmates** — actual repeated loops and information gain, but begins from a Japanese high-school class transfer, likely strongly anime/LN flavored, and therefore poor tonal fit.
- **Death Is My Checkpoint** — medieval-game setting and human male duke, but explicit Player/System/game-world framing and old-life game knowledge make it too gamey and likely power-optimization focused.
- **Sword God In Time Loop** — surfaced but insufficient trustworthy evidence of quality/fit; do not recommend without deeper verification.
