## Strong recommendation: CHAINS: The First Link
- Royal Road original, ONGOING.
- Human male lead (Damian), local protagonist, high fantasy, LitRPG-light, mystery/dungeon progression, romance only a subplot.
- 161 chapters / ~1,241 RR pages at search time; current schedule W/Th/F, next chapter Sept. 30, 2026.
- Author explicitly states no AI was used for writing or ideas.
- Opening chapters were inspected directly. The story starts with Damian leading an established adventuring party rather than being instantly proclaimed special. His abilities are useful but not overwhelming.
- The opening imposes immediate permanent consequences and makes the party fail to control the situation; the aftermath is treated seriously rather than instantly undone.
- Later review at chapter 159 says early mistakes continue to create consequences and that survival feels earned even when luck is involved.
- Other reviews repeatedly emphasize real consequences, permanent loss, character-driven stakes, and no last-second safety net.
- Strong fit signals beyond setbacks: large mystery around the world's curse, missing goddesses, political/social structures, side characters with distinct voices, and a world intended to change over time.
- Caveats: still a LitRPG/dungeon story; the opening uses frequent internal asides and light banter; there is some romance subplot. Not a time loop.

## Strong recommendation with setting caveat: Slave Origin Playthrough [Grimdark Gamelit]
- Royal Road original, ONGOING.
- Human male MC, 30-year-old gamer transported into a high-fantasy soulslike game world as a slave.
- 236 chapters / ~2,324 RR pages; updated Monday/Thursday.
- No AI-Assisted warning surfaced.
- First two chapters inspected directly. The MC has huge game knowledge, but it does NOT make him competent at actually fighting or surviving in-body. He immediately discovers that information and mechanical knowledge do not equal execution.
- A review around chapter 126 is unusually explicit: the MC does not always win or make the right choices; his mistakes have consequences; struggle/personal growth remain major parts of the story; reviewer describes it as having no plot armor.
- Strongest positive for the user's current request: sustained evidence that foreknowledge does not collapse tension.
- Caveats: much heavier GameLit/dungeon emphasis than the user's ideal, very grimdark and graphic, Asian-folklore-inspired monsters/world, and the opening contains crude humor/profanity. The author labels the path weak-to-strong-to-OP, so eventual power is expected.
