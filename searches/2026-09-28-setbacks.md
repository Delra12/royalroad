## Strong recommendation: Immovable Mage [Progression Fantasy]
- Royal Road original, ONGOING as of Sept. 28, 2026.
- Human male MC; high-fantasy progression story.
- Very large backlog: 318 chapters / ~4,420 RR pages at search time; roughly weekly cadence, with the next chapter scheduled for Oct. 3, 2026.
- The opening commits to an actual limitation: Terry repeatedly fails basic spellcasting, receives a permanent impairment diagnosis, and loses his academy place. The disability is not immediately revealed to be a secretly superior talent.
- A review written around chapter 207 explicitly says that Terry "doesn't always win" and describes ideological/political pressures that force him to grow. The same reviewer also says his plot armor can weaken the feeling of threat, so this is NOT Re:Zero/Hundred Reigns-level catastrophic repeated failure.
- Other strong fit signals: political machinations, developed supporting cast, independent ideologies, large world, background-only romance.
- Best framing for this user: regular setbacks, hard limitations, difficult choices, and occasional losses rather than a repeated-death/failure-loop story.

## Tentative wildcard: Forged in Foreign Blood [Grounded Isekai, Systemless]
- Royal Road original, ONGOING; Book 1 complete and Volume 2 underway.
- Human male MC; high-fantasy portal/military story.
- 36 chapters / ~352 pages at search time; next chapter scheduled Sept. 29, 2026.
- The author explicitly promises no plot armor, no convenient solutions, hard-earned progression, independent characters, and a protagonist with zero magical affinity in a magic-dominated world.
- An early review (chapter 6) supports the grounded/earned-struggle claim, noting that the story gives the protagonist nothing away and requires effort/ingenuity.
- Confidence is substantially lower than Immovable Mage because the story is only about one month old, has very few ratings/reviews, and there is not yet enough independent evidence that meaningful losses/setbacks persist across a long run.
- Significant user-fit caveat: it starts with an alternate-1984 veteran soldier before moving to the fantasy world, rather than beginning fully medieval; it is also grimdark/military and carries a romance-subplot tag.
- Treat as a watch/try candidate, not a high-confidence recommendation.
