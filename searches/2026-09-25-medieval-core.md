# 2026-09-25 — Medieval-core search

## New setting requirement
User strongly prefers the beginning/main world to be medieval or medieval-adjacent fantasy. Other planes/realms are fine later. Modern Earth, system-apocalypse, roguelite/tower, or sci-fi-first settings are poor fits even when the loop mechanics are good.

## Strongest new candidate
- **Re:ZERO -Starting Life in Another World-** — male MC; core setting is a fantasy kingdom with royal/noble politics, villages, mansions, knights, magic, monsters, and multiple factions. Death-triggered resets are central. Strong evidence that failed runs are used to uncover mysteries, learn different sides of conflicts/characters, and build solutions rather than simply farm stats. Subaru genuinely loses repeatedly and remains personally vulnerable. Major caveats: Japanese light-novel/anime tone, romance/emotional fixation is more prominent than the user's ideal, protagonist can be frustrating/cringe early, and it is an isekai from modern Japan even though the actual narrative world is medieval fantasy. Strong fit mechanically and in setting after arrival.

## Considered / lower fit
- **The Stubborn Skill-Grinder in a Time Loop** — male MC, fantasy setting with villages/nations/magic schools/gods and repeated deaths. However, evidence strongly suggests combat/skill grinding and 'numbers go up' are the main engine, with less mystery/strategy/character depth. Some reviews explicitly say the MC solves problems by repeatedly punching them rather than outsmarting them, and later may become too dominant. Probably a poor fit despite medieval setting and genuine repeated deaths.
- **The Jester of Apocalypse** — male MC and strong repeated-failure loop early, but the loop is largely a Book 1 phenomenon and later writing/MC behavior gets heavily criticized. Not a strong recommendation.
- **The Hedge Wizard** — male MC, very strong medieval/D&D-style fantasy setting and genuine danger/progression. Not a time loop. Early books contain losses and costly victories, but later critical reviews complain the MC trends toward solving problems via mid-fight power-ups and reduced tension. Possible setting fit, weaker loss-fit long-term.
- **Codex Alera** — male MC, Roman/medieval-adjacent secondary world, politics/war/status progression, initially underpowered. Not a loop. Potential fit for genuine adversity and politics, but progression is less central early and chosen-one concerns exist. Do not prioritize over Re:Zero.
- **The Rage of Dragons** — male MC and fantasy-medieval/African-inspired war setting with hard training and loss, but strongly fight/revenge focused; multiple reviews criticize limited mystery/character depth and later power dominance. Poorer fit.
- **Art of the Adept** — male MC, medieval fantasy, substantial genuine setbacks and an especially low later point, but repeated complaints about MC decision-making, power escalation, and controversial character choices. Not a clean recommendation.

## Search lesson
The setting filter materially changes the ranking. A mechanically excellent loop story is still a poor recommendation if its core world is modern/system-apocalypse/roguelite. Favor medieval fantasy with politics, factions, mysteries, and repeated meaningful failure over loop mechanics alone.
