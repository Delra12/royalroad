# 2026-10-01 — continued search for meaningful setbacks

## Search goal
Find new Royal Road stories with human male protagonist(s), ACTIVE/ONGOING or COMPLETED status, no AI warning, fantasy-first setting, and genuine evidence of recurring failure/setbacks rather than blurbs claiming “no plot armor.” Deduplicate against all prior repo notes.

## Strongest new candidate: Stained — T.J. Winter
- Royal Road original, ONGOING.
- Two human male protagonists (Bracken and Passage) plus an expanding multi-POV cast. This is the main caveat versus the user’s usual single-MC preference; both central leads are male.
- Native high-fantasy setting; non-LitRPG structured magic system; progression/cultivation tags.
- ~39 chapters / ~326 RR pages at search time; M/W/F schedule.
- No AI-Assisted/AI-Generated warning surfaced.
- Author explicitly promises earned weak-to-strong progression, slow-burn power growth, and power with consequences.
- Opening chapters inspected directly. The leads begin powerless, resource-poor, reckless in believable teenage ways, and are visibly outclassed by established magic users. There is no immediate legendary-bloodline, secret-god-tier-skill, prodigy-recognition, or chosen-one reveal.
- Mid-book material was inspected privately to test the premise. Their schemes can go badly wrong, objectives can fail or become pyrrhic, resources can be lost, and the fallout creates new problems rather than turning into a free power-up. Keep user-facing description spoiler-free.
- Reader reviews praise distinct side characters with their own motives/flaws, slow progression, worldbuilding that hints at a larger setting, and the absence of no-stakes OP escalation.
- Strong additional fit: criminal/faction politics, friend-group dynamics, mysteries around the magic/Planes, power affecting personality, and consequences for risky choices.
- Main caveat: only ~1–2 months old, so long-run proof is still weaker than a 200+ chapter series. Also dual protagonists rather than one.

## Secondary wildcard: The Song and the Serpent — S. L. Murphy
- Royal Road original, COMPLETED, 47 chapters / ~429 pages.
- Human male protagonist (Adan), traditional high-fantasy adventure; no LitRPG/system.
- Opening chapters inspected. Prose is straightforward/traditional and dialogue is natural enough, but it reads somewhat YA/classic-adventure in tone.
- Story quickly puts the expedition in situations where competence does not prevent capture, loss, or being outmatched; later reviews describe themes of loss and sacrifice and a world with ancient powers/history.
- However, there is not enough direct evidence that the MC himself repeatedly fails at the frequency the user currently wants. Keep as a secondary wildcard rather than a primary recommendation.
- Christian-fantasy themes are explicit in reviews/author profile; not inherently a mismatch, but worth noting if ever surfaced.
