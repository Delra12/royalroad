# 2026-10-02 — continued search for MCs who genuinely lose / face setbacks

## Search goal
Find new Royal Road stories with human male MC, ACTIVE/ONGOING or COMPLETED status, no AI warning, and meaningful recurring resistance/setbacks. Prefer fantasy-first/medieval-adjacent settings, independent side characters, and earned progression. Avoid fake weakness, chosen-one validation, obvious secret-god-tier setups, and stories where every loss becomes a power-up.

## Strongest established candidate: Brewing Bad (Fantasy Isekai Light LitRPG) — DWinchester
- Royal Road original, COMPLETED.
- Single human male MC, high-fantasy setting, weak-to-strong, crafting/alchemy, strategy, light LitRPG that increases over time.
- 187 chapters / ~1,381 RR pages.
- No AI-Assisted/AI-Generated warning; warnings are profanity and sensitive content.
- First three chapters inspected directly. Lucas begins already operating as a small-time criminal/alchemist rather than a chosen hero. He is competent in his niche but physically vulnerable, gets arrested/beaten, and must improvise rather than overpower problems.
- Long-run evidence is much stronger than most current candidates. Review at chapter 68 says the MC makes mistakes, suffers setbacks, and that side characters have their own motivations and do not always mix well. A 2026 end-of-story review describes him as balanced/realistic, making mistakes, having flaws, not knowing everything, and still growing.
- Reviews also praise plans/payoffs/consequences and describe progression as up-and-down rather than a clean escalator.
- Strong caveats for this user: Lucas is a morally ugly antihero/criminal, the story has crude/profane humor, and the premise is deliberately a fantasy riff on Breaking Bad. It is much more crime/alchemy/kingdom-building than mystery/academy/political-discovery fantasy. Some critical reviews dislike later characterization of women and call out sexism/continuity issues. Recommend as a setback-focused wildcard, not a perfect overall taste match.

## Promising but too new for high confidence: Demon Lord's Shadow [Progression Fantasy] — Brian Philip
- Royal Road original, ONGOING.
- Human male MC (Dai), native fantasy world, no LitRPG/system, magical martial arts / wuxia-flavored progression.
- 19 chapters / ~149 RR pages as of Oct. 2, 2026; current stated schedule 3 chapters/week (M/W/F), with another chapter scheduled Oct. 3.
- No AI warning surfaced.
- First three chapters inspected; later current chapters/reviews checked privately for fit. The inciting catastrophe is directly caused by Dai's own selfish/bad decision, and the story explicitly refuses to excuse him for it. He carries guilt rather than receiving a compensating chosen-one reward.
- Early reviews repeatedly note that Dai struggles with training/meditation, is genuinely frightened, is not instantly confident or overpowered, and must learn that brute force does not solve everything.
- Current later chapters still show injury, being outplayed, and dangerous situations rather than clean domination. This is encouraging but only verifies the first ~19 chapters; not enough material exists yet to prove sustained setbacks across a long series.
- Prose: clean/concrete overall; early reviews criticize repetitive short declarative sentences in chapters 1–3, but say this improves around chapter 4.
- Main caveat: far too young to certify as a long-run failure-heavy story. Recommend as a try/watch candidate, not at the confidence level of an established serial.
